# CreatorVault

## Overview

CreatorVault is a web-based creator marketing platform that connects content creators with brands for successful marketing collaborations.

The platform allows brands to discover creators, create campaigns, manage applications, track campaign performance, and evaluate creators through a transparent Trust Score system.

Creators can build professional profiles, apply for campaigns, showcase their work, and grow their reputation through verified collaborations.

---

## Problem Statement

Brands often struggle to find reliable creators for marketing campaigns, while creators find it difficult to discover quality collaboration opportunities.

Current processes rely heavily on manual communication through social media platforms, resulting in:

- Time-consuming creator discovery
- Lack of trust and transparency
- Poor campaign tracking
- No centralized collaboration platform

CreatorVault solves these challenges by providing a dedicated marketplace for creators and brands.
